Privacy Policy

App: MyPepDoc  ·  Last updated: October 27, 2026

About MyPepDoc

MyPepDoc is an educational and informational tracking application for peptides. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Most of the peptides referenced in the app are NOT FDA-approved medications for human therapeutic use.

Any dose ranges and schedules shown within the app or by the AI characters are what published research or product labeling reports, shared for educational purposes only — they are not prescriptions or recommendations. Any figures the app shows from vial, diluent, or dose values you enter are simple arithmetic on your own entries — the app does not determine, prescribe, or recommend what amount you should take.

⚠️ Use at Your Own Risk

USE AT YOUR OWN RISK. Use of any peptide is entirely at the user’s own risk. MyPepDoc, CodeByJ, and all associated developers, designers, and contributors assume no liability whatsoever for any health outcomes, adverse effects, injuries, or consequences arising from the use or misuse of any substance referenced in this application. Always consult a licensed healthcare provider.

Users acknowledge and accept full personal responsibility for any decisions made based on information provided within MyPepDoc. The AI doctor characters (Dr. Lexi and Dr. Rax) are fictional characters powered by artificial intelligence — they are NOT licensed physicians or medical professionals and their communications do not constitute medical advice.

MyPepDoc is built with a simple principle: your data stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no login, and no server of ours, and it does not transmit your information to us or to anyone else. This page explains exactly what the app stores, what it doesn’t, and how you can delete it.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

MyPepDoc stores the following information only on your device:

  • Your first name and gender
  • Your chosen AI doctor character
  • Reminder preferences
  • Peptide protocol entries — peptide name, schedule, the vial, diluent, and dose values you enter, and your notes
  • Dose logs and history

This information never leaves your device through the app. CodeByJ does not receive it, cannot see it, and has no server-side database of your personal information. We do not collect your email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, or health records.

2. AI Doctor Characters — Answers Generated On Your Device

Dr. Lexi and Dr. Rax are AI characters, not licensed physicians. Their answers are produced on your device, using Apple’s on-device language model on devices that support it, or from the app’s built-in research library otherwise.

Your questions stay on your device The questions you ask Dr. Lexi or Dr. Rax, and the answers they give, are never transmitted to CodeByJ or to any outside server or AI provider.
What MyPepDoc does NOT do
  • No accounts, login, or contact information
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No collecting, selling, or sharing of personal data
  • No network requests of its own (see section 3)

3. Network Activity

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only networking is Apple’s StoreKit, which Apple uses to process subscriptions. Links to outside websites — such as the FDA, DailyMed, PubMed, and ClinicalTrials.gov — open in your web browser only when you tap them; those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Reminders and Notifications

Dose reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

6. No Analytics, Advertising, or Data Sales

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone.

7. Subscriptions and Purchases

All purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

8. Deleting Your Data

You have full control over everything MyPepDoc stores.

  • Delete all app data: Go to Settings > Reset App to permanently delete your profile, protocol entries, logs, and preferences. This cannot be undone.
  • Delete the app: Uninstalling MyPepDoc removes all of its locally stored data. We have no server-side accounts, so there is nothing else for us to delete.

Device backups

Depending on your device settings, MyPepDoc’s data may be included in your own device backups (iCloud or your computer). Those backups are controlled by you and your backup settings, not by CodeByJ, and restoring from a backup may restore that data.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your device’s built-in security, such as iOS data protection when a passcode is set. Because the app does not transmit your personal data, we operate no servers storing it.

10. Children’s Privacy

MyPepDoc is rated 17+ and is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 17, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.
